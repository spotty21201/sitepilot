# Product gaps and next phase

The Taskmaster three-scheme workflow, deterministic planning engine, human approval boundary, and grounded post-simulation assessment are implemented. Deferred work is intentionally outside the hackathon candidate:

1. verified cadastral, municipal, market, cost, and commercial data integrations;
2. account-backed, collaborative project persistence and access control;
3. controlled evidence/document ingestion and durable artifact storage;
4. broader real-project validation with professional planners and designers;
5. production retention, privacy, recovery, and observability policies;
6. owner licensing, eligibility, rights, video publication, and Devpost submission actions.

No deferred feature should be described as part of the demonstrated product.
